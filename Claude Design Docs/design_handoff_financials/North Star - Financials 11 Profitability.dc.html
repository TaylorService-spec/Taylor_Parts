Margin cannot be reported yet.
Gross margin is revenue minus cost. Revenue facts are governed today; cost has no authority until FIN-006 — so EOS declines to draw a margin rather than draw a wrong one. When cost authority lands, this page reports Revenue, Cost, Gross Margin and GM% across every dimension at left.
