Sales to Goal

Actuals against governed goals. Each goal names its measurement basis.iBasis is one of BOOKED / BILLED / COLLECTED / REVENUE / GROSS_MARGIN, stated on the goal record and repeated in every row here. Rows with different bases never roll into one total — rollups group by basis and say so. GROSS_MARGIN-based goals render an unavailable state until FIN-006 establishes margin authority.
