# Davisware / ECI Software Solutions

**Last researched:** 2026-08-16 · **By:** field-service-domain-researcher (round 1)

**Why this matters:** Davisware (GlobalEdge, Vision) is purpose-built for **commercial food
equipment service** — our exact niche — and was a complete blind spot until this pass.
**ECI Software Solutions acquired Davisware in April 2025**
([BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20250411297322/en/ECI-Software-Solutions-Acquires-Davisware-Expanding-Reach-and-Innovation-in-Field-Service-Management)).
Treat them as the **incumbent of record** in this segment.

## 1. Capability claims

From [ECI's commercial food equipment page](https://www.ecisolutions.com/industries/field-service/commercial-food-and-equipment/):
scheduling/dispatch with technician matching by location, availability and skill · inventory
management · invoicing and payments · job costing · mobile field app · **equipment/asset history
for repair-vs-replace decisions** · **fleet tracking including truck contents** · **PartsPath**
parts ordering via a Partstown partnership (an integration, not a built-in catalog).

From [the Vision page](https://www.ecisolutions.com/products/vision/): customer data, service
history, equipment records and **PM agreements in one place**.

**Warranty is a separate product** — Global Warranty — not folded into the core.

**Multi-branch** is claimed explicitly: *"Seamlessly run multiple branches or locations on a
single integrated system."* Note carefully: **multi-branch is not multi-legal-entity.**

## 2. Pricing — none published

Both product pages show only "Get Pricing" / "Get a Demo." Software Advice confirms: *"Pricing is
'available upon request' with no standard plans listed. No free trial or free version is
offered."* ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/product/131494-Davisware/))

An aggregator estimate of **$150–$300/user/month** exists
([SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/field-service/davisware-llc)) but is explicitly
labelled as industry-benchmark modelling, not vendor-confirmed. **Treat every dollar figure here
as ASSUMPTION-grade.**

## 3. Lived experience

Positive clusters: dispatch/field workflow and paperless operation; support quality; several
7+-year customers. Negative clusters: **update instability** — *"updates that fix one problem and
introduce two more are all too common"* · **inconsistent support responsiveness** (directly
contradicting the positive support cluster — both exist, both recorded) · **rigid reporting** —
*"Unless you have a report customized, it's hard to get the information you need"*, with one
review flagging financial statements as unreliable · cost-versus-utilisation dissatisfaction.
Roughly 4.0–4.1/5 across ~27–40 reviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/119745/Davisware/reviews/)) —
**a small sample; treat cautiously.**

## 4. Multi-entity — UNRESOLVED, and it matters most

A search-index version of the GlobalEdge page stated it supports *"managing multiple entities
within one database, managing more than one balance sheet, and managing more than one income
statement."* **Fetching that exact URL directly returned different content** discussing
multi-*location* warehouses and teams, not multi-entity accounting.

That discrepancy is real, not noise — a cached older page, or a sub-page not independently
located.

**Confirmed consistently:** multi-branch/multi-location on one system.
**Found nowhere, in any version:** intercompany transfer pricing, or combined/consolidated
reporting across separate legal entities.

**Do not treat this as settled either way.** Next step is a direct ECI spec sheet or demo of the
GlobalEdge accounting module, or a targeted search for `"GlobalEdge" + "consolidated financials"`
against Davisware-hosted PDFs.

## 5. CRM

Davisware claims lead/opportunity/deal tracking with *"visibility over their forecasts and sales
funnels"* (davisware.com/sales-marketing, captured via search index; the live page now redirects).
The current Vision page emphasises service-context customer data rather than pipeline.

**Net read:** CRM exists as an adjacent module; service/dispatch/inventory/accounting is the
centre of gravity. No review evidence discusses CRM quality, so **"exists" cannot be upgraded to
"works well."** Do not assume they are CRM-weak.

## 6. The Middleby partnership — weaker than hypothesised

**This is a warranty-administration relationship, not dealer-network distribution.** Middleby has
been *"a loyal Global Warranty customer of Davisware for more than two decades"*; the 2023
announcement **extended Global Warranty to additional Middleby brands**
([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230927448282/en/Davisware-Expands-its-Partnership-with-Middleby-Corporation)).
Global Warranty *"manages over 11 million pieces of equipment and collaborates with 22,000
service agents."*

That is a **manufacturer using warranty-claims software to run its own OEM warranty program.**

**No source states Middleby requires or recommends Davisware's field-service ERP to its dealer
network.** The earlier "manufacturer-driven rollout" hypothesis is **not supported** by this
evidence.

## 7. What this changes for us

- **Do not claim "Davisware can't do multi-entity"** until §4 is resolved. It may be our wedge; it
  may already be theirs.
- **Equipment/asset history plus PM agreements are table stakes** in this niche — our Equipment
  Custody / Serialized Asset work must at minimum match that bar.
- **Do not assume CRM is a differentiator versus them.** Verify depth, not existence.
- **Reporting rigidity and update instability are their repeated, sourced weaknesses** — a bar to
  hold ourselves to, and a fair comparison point once we have live customers.
- **Treat them as the incumbent of record** for RFPs in commercial foodservice equipment service.

## 8. ASSUMPTIONS

- Aggregator pricing ($150–300/user/month; $10K–150K implementation) reflects real market pricing.
- The multi-entity accounting claim is accurate for GlobalEdge — sourced only via search
  summarization, not independently re-verifiable.
