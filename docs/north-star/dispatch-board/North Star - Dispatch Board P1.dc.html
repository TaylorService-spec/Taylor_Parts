Board rules (unchanged authority)

A drop proposes the governed transition through the existing engine — the board is a faster hand, not a new authority. Illegal moves (ineligible status, outside a shift, onto blocked time) are refused and the chip springs back with the reason in words. Reassigning off a scheduled technician demands a typed reason (1b). Dispatched chips are facts, not drag handles, until a governed Reschedule exists (ND-3/B1).
