1dHonest states & identifier surface
Blocked — permission. “You do not have access to the canonical Parts catalog.” Detail never falls back to static data; a blocked read blocks the page (the domain’s own fail-closed rule).
Blocked — unavailable / unverified. “The canonical Parts catalog is currently unavailable” vs “This part could not be verified against the canonical source, so its details are not shown.” Distinct from Not found.
Not found. A legitimate “no such part” — different sentence, different fix, never conflated with a blocked read.
Zero inventory, valid part. The record renders fully; Where-it-is says “No units on hand anywhere — last movement Aug 2 (scrapped).” A part with no stock is not a missing part.
Capability inactive. Sections whose read exists but isn’t granted/active render in place: “Serialized-asset detail isn’t available to you.” Structure preserved, nothing simulated.
Identifier surface (existing, governed). Alias list with type words (“Manufacturer part number”, “UPC barcode”…), active/inactive state, add/deactivate/reactivate through the governed commands, and scan-to-test with its five honest outcomes — “resolves to THIS part” / “a DIFFERENT part” / “registered but INACTIVE” / “nothing registered” / “not a usable value”. Aliases and barcodes never become canonical identity.
