The seven questions

1 · Archetype. Overview. The board schedules, the record executes, Operations owns inventory — this page owns the cross-cutting exceptions read. Survives as a destination (SO-D1, Owner ratifies; delete remains on the table if the Owner prefers the board absorb it).
2 · First screen. Attention block, then the four linked numbers. A dispatcher sees every open decision without scrolling.
3 · Work-order list. No. Exceptions render as 32px table rows; the full list is the Work Orders family's job.
4 · Six panels. Two earn the field of view (Attention, At Risk). One is the suggestion tray (Dispatch Queue). Two fold or demote (Overloaded → load column, Activity → rail). One leaves (Parts Overview).
5 · Metrics. Awaiting dispatch / In progress / Technicians on shift / Completed — each with exception count and destination. Never five tiles, never unlinked.
6 · Attention. One surface. The ⚠ warning becomes its Urgent section; renders nothing when clean.
7 · Actions. Off the cards, off this page. Governed transitions live on the record and the board; the header's cluster is navigational, with Open Dispatch Board as the one filled primary.
