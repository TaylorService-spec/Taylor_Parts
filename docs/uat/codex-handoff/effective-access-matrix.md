# C. Effective-access matrix (EXPECTED)

> **Requires #2201 (persona harness target allowlist) merged and nonprod-verified before any Codex run.** Generated by `docs/uat/tools/uatCensusGenerate.mjs` from the nonprod census exported 2026-10-21 and the repository at `b433d396`. Names only: no credential, token or secret appears here.

EXPECTED is derived **only** from governed configuration (census `role_capabilities`, `role_object_permissions`, scoped assignments, the surface catalog, company authority) plus the Owner rulings cited per row. Where configuration and a ruling disagree, or nothing establishes the expectation, the cell is **UNDETERMINED**, is listed as an Owner question and is **never asserted**. After the Owner's 2026-10-21 decisions (`../07-owner-questions.md`) there are **0** such cells. No permission is inferred to make a test pass.

The complete machine-readable matrix is `codex-handoff/expected-access-manifest.json` (13107 rows). This page explains it and shows the headline views.

## C0. Method and authority order

1. **Capability** (`role_capabilities` of the held Roles, plus the derived protected-Administrator standing) decides every command. A scoped Role assignment confers the capability only inside its scope (sales channel / operating company) and never behaves as global.
2. **Company authority** (`companyAuthority.ts`): foreign-company Commercial records (Q-1a) are NONEXISTENT with zero trace; the protected Administrator is unrestricted for Commercial; the protected Owner follows its own companies (the Owner persona holds both through `grantCompanyAuthority`); inventory follows the governed company list; Finance follows the explicit Financials company assignment.
3. **Channel / ownership / assignment / warehouse scope** narrow within a company.
4. A **workflow binding never grants** (it can only narrow a capability).
5. **Field visibility** inherits the Object read unless a Role field override exists; a field grant never opens an Object (the doorway invariant).
6. **Object CRED** (`role_object_permissions`) is a stored projection of the Firebase-era catalog; the EOS commands decide on capabilities. CRED is tested as the Administration Roles view (readback) and its divergence from capabilities is recorded, not asserted as a runtime expectation (OQ-01).

## C8. Channel boundary (OQ-10): Retail and National Accounts are distinct Job Roles

Both channel-scoped personas hold the same Security Roles (`salesperson` scoped by channel plus `commercialSharedContext`), so the separation is carried entirely by the channel restriction. The `channel-boundary` tier proves it for each persona and each channel through SEARCH, LIST, DETAIL and the direct API by id: the persona's own channel is SCOPED (visible), the other channel is DENY (identical not-found; a write is refused `OUTSIDE_SALES_CHANNEL_SCOPE` after the company check). National Accounts is a channel, never a company. 48 rows.

## C9. Shared customer Accounts (OQ-07)

An Account is readable across Taylor and Ventana with the existing Account authorization (`customer.record.read`) and never grants the other company's Commercial or Finance records: a Taylor-only person may read a Ventana-line Account's name and contacts and sees none of Ventana's Opportunities, Agreements, Orders, Work Orders or receivables. Record kind `V-ACC` rows are ALLOW for holders of the read; every other Ventana kind is DENY without Ventana company authority.
