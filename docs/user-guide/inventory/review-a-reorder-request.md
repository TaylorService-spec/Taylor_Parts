# Review a reorder request (approve or reject)

**What this lets you do:** Decide whether a submitted reorder request should proceed. Approving hands it off to the Parts Manager; rejecting closes it with a reason.

**Who can do it:** Administrators and Dispatchers.

## Before you start
- Open the request from a notification, or from **Inventory > Parts** click the part name in any queue, or open the part's detail page directly.

## Steps
1. On the part's detail page, find the **Reorder Request -- Pending Review** card. It shows the requested quantity, recommendation status, recommended quantity, and the risk at request time.
2. To approve, click **Approve**. The request moves to the **Parts Manager Queue** for assignment.
3. To reject, click **Reject**, type your reason in **Review notes (required to reject)**, then click **Confirm Rejection**. A reason is required — the confirm button stays disabled until you enter one.

## Tips and common problems
- **No review card appears:** the request has already been decided or has moved further along. You'll see its current status card instead.
- **Error message on the card:** the action didn't go through; the message explains why and nothing changed.
