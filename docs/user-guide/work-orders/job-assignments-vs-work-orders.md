# Job Assignments vs. Work Orders

Under **Service**, you'll see two related screens. This note explains the
difference so you always open the right one. (Nothing in the app was removed —
only a heading was made clearer.)

## What changed

The screen at **Service › Job Assignments** used to show the heading **"Work
Orders."** That was misleading — it is not the main Work Orders workspace. Its
heading now reads **"Job Assignments,"** matching its place in the menu and its
**+ New Job** button. No buttons, data, or steps changed.

## Which screen do I want?

| If you want to… | Go to |
|-----------------|-------|
| Create and manage **Work Orders** (the customer-facing service records) | **Service › Work Orders** |
| Work with individual **job assignments** (the older per-job list) | **Service › Job Assignments** |

- **Service › Work Orders** is the main workspace for Work Orders — browse, search,
  open a Work Order's detail, and (where enabled) create one with the Work Order
  wizard.
- **Service › Job Assignments** is the older, simpler per-job list. It still works
  and is still available; it just no longer *calls itself* "Work Orders."

## Why this matters

Having two screens both titled "Work Orders" made it easy to open the wrong one.
The clearer heading means the title on screen now always tells you which one
you're on. If you had a bookmark or habit built around the old "Work Orders"
heading under Job Assignments, the screen is the same — only its title changed.
