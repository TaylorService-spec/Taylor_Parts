# Progress your assigned work order (technician)

**What this lets you do:** Move a work order that's been dispatched to you through your side of the job — accept it, travel, arrive, start work, and complete it.

**Who can do it:** Technicians. You only ever see work orders assigned to you.

> **Not yet available.** The buttons appear on your assigned work orders, but the service that records each step isn't switched on in the live app. Pressing a button shows an error and the status doesn't change. This guide documents the flow so it's ready for when it's turned on.

## Before you start

- Sign in with your technician account.
- A dispatcher must have dispatched a work order to you for it to appear.

## Steps

1. Open **My Dashboard**. Your assigned work orders are listed there.
2. Open a work order to see its status and the action button for your next step. Only the valid next step is shown:
   - **Accept** — you've been dispatched; accept the job.
   - **Start Travel** — you're on your way.
   - **Arrived On Site** — you've reached the site.
   - **Start Work** — you're beginning the job.
   - **Complete Work Order** — the job is done.
3. Tap the button. The status advances to the next stage (once the service is live).

## Tips and common problems

- **No action button, just a status.** If the work order is still with the dispatcher (not yet dispatched to you), or already completed/closed/cancelled, there's nothing for you to do on it right now.
- You'll only ever see your own assignments — work orders for other technicians don't appear on your dashboard.
