# Field Ops user guide

Task-oriented how-to guides for people using the Field Ops app — not architecture docs. Organized by
what you're trying to do, in plain language, matching the app's actual on-screen wording.

## Technician (phone)

- [Working with no signal](./working-with-no-signal.md) — recording notes, time, parts and
  installations offline; how to tell what's outstanding; resolving conflicts; finishing an
  installation job offline; dictation vs. typing; signing out with work waiting.

## Dispatch

- [Schedule, move and dispatch on the Dispatcher Board](./dispatch/dispatch-a-work-order-on-the-dispatcher-board.md)
  — scheduling from the queue, drag and keyboard moves, reassigning, returning to the queue,
  dispatching; the business time zone line; what you see without scheduling access.
- [Schedule and dispatch jobs (Jobs screen)](./dispatch/assign-a-job-on-the-dispatch-screen.md) —
  schedule, move, reassign and dispatch from the Jobs list.
- [Jobs and the board share one scheduling path](./dispatch/schedule-and-dispatch-from-jobs-and-the-board.md)
  — what is the same on both screens, working hours being enforced, and which company information you
  see (and which you do not).
- [Read the Dispatch & Scheduling board](./dispatch/read-the-dispatch-board.md) — lane colours, time zone,
  keeping your place.
- [Which work orders you can see](./work-orders/which-work-orders-you-can-see.md) — work orders belong to
  one operating company: what is hidden, why a missing work order is not an error, and work done for another
  company's sale.

## Inventory and scanning

- [The Scan workspace](./inventory/scan-workspace.md) — including why stock balance rows say
  "Not switched on".
- [View and manage manufacturers](./inventory/view-manufacturers.md) — see the list, add a manufacturer,
  correct a name, and make one Active or Inactive.
- [Change a part's status](./inventory/change-a-part-status.md) — who can move a part to its next
  allowed status, and how it differs from supplier status.

## Customers

- [Browse and search customers](./accounts-customers/browse-and-search-customers.md) — name search,
  the status filter, and why the portfolio totals are not shown yet.
- [Record customer activity and notes](./accounts-customers/record-customer-activity-and-notes.md) — the Activity & Notes log:
  who can see it, and adding a call, meeting or note.

## Sales

- [Record a Sales Agreement](./sales/record-a-sales-agreement.md) — writing down what the customer
  agreed to, pricing the lines, editing the draft while you negotiate, and accepting it. An accepted
  agreement is what a Sales Order gets its prices from.
- [Find an opportunity](./sales/find-an-opportunity.md) — the Opportunities list: the views along
  the top, searching and filtering, what each column means, and opening a deal.
- [Edit an opportunity](./sales/edit-an-opportunity.md)
- [Mark an opportunity won](./sales/mark-an-opportunity-won.md)
- [Allocate stock to a Sales Order](./sales/allocate-stock-to-a-sales-order.md) — set aside a line's
  stock at a warehouse you choose, give it back, and what the system refuses.
- [Which sales records you can see](./sales/which-sales-records-you-can-see.md) — records belong to one
  operating company: what is hidden, why a missing deal is not an error, and records with no company yet.

## Administration

- [Import data from a file](./administration/import-data-from-a-file.md) — loading your existing
  Parts, Customers, Equipment, stock counts and past service records from a spreadsheet: what each
  kind of import does, what it refuses and why, and what it will never do. Sandbox only.
- [Set up warehouse racking](./administration/set-up-warehouse-racking.md) — describing a
  warehouse's aisles, bays and positions once and creating all of its bins in one pass.
- [Build and save a report](./administration/build-and-save-a-report.md) — Reporting → Report Builder
  and Saved Reports: choosing customers, contacts, locations or equipment, columns, filters, grouping
  and sorting; saving, renaming, duplicating and deleting; what you can and cannot see in a report.
- [Administration: Policy and Access](../training/administration-policy-and-access.md) — the canonical
  Administration guide: Security Roles on the Employee record, Object Security, Effective Access, Direct
  Exceptions, Functional Roles, workflows, the protected Owner and the Owner's Administrator staffing.
- [Manage users](./administration/manage-employees.md) — finding people and keeping their Employee details
  correct.
- [View an Employee's work schedule](./administration/view-an-employee-work-schedule.md) — the Work Schedule tab on an Employee record (read-only; changing hours is not switched on yet)
- [Give someone Finance access to a company](./administration/give-finance-access-to-a-company.md) — a Role's "Finance access only" setting, so a Finance company assignment does not add the company to everyday work
- [Give an Employee access to another company](./administration/give-an-employee-access-to-another-company.md) — explicit company access for one Employee: reach to a company, no permissions, no Finance access
- [Integrations](./administration/integrations-guide.md)

## Invoicing

- [Issue an invoice](./financials/issue-an-invoice.md) — **Financials > Receivables**: turn a receivable that is
  ready to bill into an invoice with its own number; who can do it, how the due date is set, and what each refusal means.
- [Correct an issued invoice](./financials/correct-an-invoice.md) — credits, write-offs, adjustments, refunds and voids: one person
  asks, a different Employee approves (even for one cent); a refund is recorded, never paid; a void only before any money moves.

## Goals

- [Performance goals](./performance-goals.md) — where a target appears next to a figure, what "No
  target has been set", "outside your access" and "could not be read" each mean, and which targets you
  can see.
- [Set, approve, replace and retire a goal](./analysis/set-approve-and-retire-goals.md) — **Analysis > Goals**:
  set a target as a draft, have someone else approve it, replace it with one that starts later, or retire it;
  who can do each step and what each refusal means.

More guides will be added here as they're written. If a guide references a screen or control that
isn't live yet, it says so rather than describing something you can't actually do.
