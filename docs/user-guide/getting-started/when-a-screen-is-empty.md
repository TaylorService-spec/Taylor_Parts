# When a screen is empty

**Status: live**

**What this lets you do:** Tell the difference between a screen with nothing in it yet and a screen whose
filters are hiding everything — and, on a brand-new screen, understand what it's for before you have any
data in it.

**Who can do it:** Everyone. This applies across the app, not to one screen.

## The two kinds of empty

Field Ops deliberately never says just "no results," because that would hide which of two very different
situations you're in:

- **Nothing exists yet.** No record of this kind has ever been created. You'll see a short title like "No
  work orders yet" and a line telling you what will appear here.
- **Your filters are hiding everything.** Records do exist, but none match what you've selected. You'll see
  wording that points at the filter — for example "No purchase orders match this filter." Clear or change
  the filter above the list and they come back.

If you're not sure which you're looking at, check the filter bar at the top of the list. If a filter other
than **All** is selected, try **All** first.

## The guidance line

On the first kind of empty only — nothing exists yet — some screens show an extra sentence or two beside a
bronze line. This explains **what the screen is for and what causes something to show up on it**. For
example, on an empty Transfers screen it tells you that transfers record stock moving between locations, and
that they're created by background processes rather than typed in on that screen — so you know the empty
list is normal and not something you've done wrong.

Two things worth knowing about it:

- **It only appears when the screen has never had any records.** Once you have data, it stops appearing. You
  won't see it again every time you filter.
- **It explains the screen, not your permissions.** If you think you should be seeing records and don't, the
  guidance line is not the place that will tell you why — ask your administrator whether your role has
  access to that area.
