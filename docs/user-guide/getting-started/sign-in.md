# Sign in to Field Ops

**What this lets you do:** Get into the Field Ops app with your work account.

**Who can do it:** Anyone with a Field Ops account (admin, dispatcher, or technician).

## Before you start
- You need the email address and password for your Field Ops account. These are set up for you by an administrator — the sign-in screen has no "create account" or "sign up" option.
- If you don't have a password yet, or forgot it, contact your administrator. There is no self-service password reset on this screen.

## Steps
1. Open the Field Ops web address. You'll see a panel titled **Field Ops Login** with two boxes and a **Sign In** button.
2. Type your work email into the **Email** box.
3. Type your password into the **Password** box.
4. Click **Sign In**. The button changes to "Signing in…" while it works.
5. When sign-in succeeds, the login panel is replaced by the app — a top bar with **Field Ops Platform**, your email on the right, and the navigation tabs for your role.

## Tips / common problems
- **"Invalid email or password."** appears under the form if the email or password is wrong. Re-type both carefully (watch for caps lock) and try again.
- **"No access" screen after signing in** — the message reads *"Your account isn't assigned a role yet. Contact an admin to get access."* This means your login worked but your account hasn't been given a role. Contact your administrator; you can't fix this yourself.
- **"DEMO MODE ACTIVE" banner** — if a yellow banner says the app is in demo mode, nothing you do will be saved. That's expected on demo links; use your normal work link for real work.
- Your email address is always shown in the top-right corner, so you can confirm which account you're signed in as.
