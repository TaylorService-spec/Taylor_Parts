# Understand your landing screen

**What this lets you do:** Know what you're looking at the moment you sign in.

**Who can do it:** Everyone — what you see depends on your role.

## Before you start
- Sign in first (see `sign-in.md`).
- After signing in, the app always opens on the **Dashboard** area, on the **My Dashboard** view.

## What you see
Every signed-in screen has the same frame:
- **Top bar:** "Field Ops Platform" on the left, with **Home** and **Refresh** links; your email address and a **Logout** button on the right.
- **Navigation tabs** below that (the business areas you're allowed to see).
- **Main area** showing the current screen.

Your landing view, **My Dashboard**, differs by role:

### Technicians
My Dashboard is your personal work view — your technician dashboard loads here automatically. This is your home base for your assigned work.

### Admins and dispatchers
My Dashboard shows a placeholder that reads *"A personalized dashboard for this role isn't built yet — see Operations Dashboard for the current admin/dispatcher view."* A personalized dashboard for these roles is **not yet available**. For your working overview, go to **Dashboard > Operations Dashboard**, which is a real screen.

## Steps
1. Sign in. The app lands on **Dashboard > My Dashboard**.
2. Look at the top-right corner to confirm you're signed in as the right person (your email is shown there).
3. Technicians: your dashboard is already in front of you. Admins/dispatchers: click the **Operations Dashboard** sub-tab under Dashboard for your live overview.

## Tips / common problems
- Clicking **Home** in the top bar always brings you back to this Dashboard landing view.
- **Refresh** reloads the whole app from scratch — use it if a screen looks stuck.
- If you only see a "No access" message instead of a dashboard, your account has no role yet — contact an admin.
