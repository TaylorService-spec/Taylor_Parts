# Understand your landing screen

**What this lets you do:** Know what you're looking at the moment you sign in.

**Who can do it:** Everyone — what you see depends on your role.

## Before you start
- Sign in first (see `sign-in.md`).
- After signing in, the app always opens on the **Dashboard** area, on the **My Dashboard** view.

## What you see
Every signed-in screen has the same frame:
- **Navigation rail** down the left side — the business areas you're allowed to see, each with its own icon.
- **A thin strip at the top of the main area** with a notification bell (if your role has anything to be notified about), your email address, and a **Logout** button.
- **Main area** showing the current screen.

Your landing view, **My Dashboard**, differs by role:

### Technicians
My Dashboard is your personal work view — your technician dashboard loads here automatically. This is your home base for your assigned work.

### Admins and dispatchers
My Dashboard shows **"What needs you right now"** — a set of cards grouped by business area (Customers, Service, Inventory, and so on), each card linking straight to one destination you can actually open. This list is built from your own access: it never shows a destination your account can't open, and it never hides one you can. Two people with different roles or different granted permissions can see genuinely different cards here — that's expected, not a bug.

If your account currently has no access to any business area, you'll see a message telling you nothing is available yet and suggesting you ask an administrator to check your role. This can happen to a real account that hasn't been assigned a role.

## Steps
1. Sign in. The app lands on **Dashboard > My Dashboard**.
2. Look at the top-right corner of the header strip to confirm you're signed in as the right person (your email is shown there).
3. Technicians: your dashboard is already in front of you. Admins/dispatchers: click any card under "What needs you right now" to jump straight to that area.

## Tips / common problems
- There's no separate "Home" or "Refresh" button anymore. To come back here, choose **Dashboard** in the navigation rail. Your browser's Back button works normally, and its refresh will reload a screen that looks stuck.
- If your landing screen looks sparse compared to a coworker's, that's normal — it reflects your own role and permissions, not a bug.
- If you see a "No access" message instead of a dashboard, your account has no role yet — contact an admin and give them the email address shown on that screen.
