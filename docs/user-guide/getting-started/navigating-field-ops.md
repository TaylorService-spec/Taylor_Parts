# Move around the app

**What this lets you do:** Get from one business area to another using the top tabs and sub-tabs.

**Who can do it:** Everyone. The tabs you see depend on your role.

## Before you start
- Sign in first (see `sign-in.md`).

## How navigation is laid out
- The **top row of tabs** is the business areas (for example Dashboard, Customers, Service, Inventory, Purchasing, Reporting, Administration). You only see the areas your role is allowed into.
- When you click a top tab, a **second row of sub-tabs** appears underneath for the sections inside that area.
- Your browser's **Back** and **Forward** buttons work normally, and each screen has its own web address you can bookmark or share.

## What each role sees
- **Admin:** the fullest set of areas — Dashboard, Customers, Service, Inventory, Purchasing, Reporting, Administration, and the planned Sales / CRM and Financials areas.
- **Dispatcher:** similar to admin (Dashboard, Customers, Service, Inventory, Purchasing, Reporting, Administration), matching a dispatcher's day-to-day scope.
- **Technician:** a deliberately narrow set focused on your own work — Dashboard and Service. Areas like Inventory, Customers, and Administration are hidden for technicians on purpose.

## Steps
1. Click a **top tab** (for example **Service**). The main area switches to that area, and its sub-tabs appear.
2. Click a **sub-tab** (for example **Dispatch** or **Work Orders**) to open that section.
3. Use the browser **Back** button to return to where you were, or click **Home** in the top bar to jump back to your Dashboard.

## Tips / common problems
- **Some sub-tabs open a "This area isn't built yet" page.** That's expected. The navigation shows the platform's full planned shape, but many sections are placeholders that are **not yet available** — for example most of Reporting, several Inventory sections (Warehouses, Transfers, Receiving, Cycle Counts, Back Orders), most of Purchasing, and most of Administration. Sections that are live today include Customers, Work Orders, Dispatch, Parts, and the Operations Dashboard.
- **A tab you expected is missing.** Tabs are filtered by role. If you can't see an area you need, your role may not include it — contact an admin.
- Typing a web address for an area your role can't reach sends you back to the Dashboard rather than showing an error.
