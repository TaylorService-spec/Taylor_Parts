# Move around the app

**What this lets you do:** Get from one business area to another using the navigation rail.

**Who can do it:** Everyone. The areas you see depend on your role.

## Before you start
- Sign in first (see `sign-in.md`).

## How navigation is laid out
- A **navigation rail** runs down the left side of the app. Each row is a business area (for example Dashboard, CRM/Sales, Service, Inventory, Purchasing, Reporting, Administration), each with its own small icon. You only see the areas your role is allowed into.
- Click an area's row to expand it and reveal the destinations inside it. Clicking the row itself only opens or closes that list — it doesn't take you anywhere. An area with just one destination (like Equipment) skips the expand step and takes you straight there.
- Inside a larger area — Service is the main example — destinations are further grouped under small headings (for example Work Management, Dispatch, Technician Workspace) so a long list stays organized.
- On a phone or a narrow browser window, the rail becomes a menu: tap the button in the top-left corner of the header strip to open it, pick a destination, and it closes automatically.
- Your browser's **Back** and **Forward** buttons work normally, and each screen has its own web address you can bookmark or share.

## What each role sees
- **Admin and Dispatcher:** the fullest set of areas — Dashboard, CRM/Sales, Equipment, Service, Service Operations, Inventory, Purchasing, Reporting, and Administration.
- **Technician:** a deliberately narrow set focused on your own work — Dashboard and Service. Areas like Inventory, CRM/Sales, and Administration are hidden for technicians on purpose. A technician who also holds an additional inventory role (Parts Manager, Warehouse Manager, or Parts Associate) will also see a **My Inventory Role** area with just their own destination inside it.

## Steps
1. Click an area's row in the rail (for example **Service**) to expand it.
2. Click a destination inside it (for example **Dispatch Queue** or **Work Orders**) to open that screen.
3. Use the browser **Back** button to return to where you were, or click a different area's row to jump elsewhere.

## Tips / common problems
- **Some destinations open a "This area isn't built yet" page.** That's expected. The navigation shows the platform's full planned shape, but a few sections are still placeholders that are **not yet available**. Sections that are live today include CRM/Sales, Work Orders, Dispatch, Parts, and the areas covered by the other guides in this section.
- **A destination you expected is missing.** Destinations are filtered by role (and sometimes by a specific granted permission). If you can't see an area you need, your role may not include it — contact an admin.
- Typing a web address for an area your role can't reach sends you back to the Dashboard rather than showing an error.
- The rail can stay open on more than one area at once — opening a new one doesn't close the last one you had open, so you can keep two related areas (like Inventory and Purchasing) both expanded at the same time.
