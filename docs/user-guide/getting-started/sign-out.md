# Sign out of Field Ops

**What this lets you do:** End your session and return to the login screen — important on shared computers.

**Who can do it:** Anyone who is signed in.

## Before you start
- Nothing needed. The **Logout** button is in the top-right corner of every screen.

## Steps
1. Find your email address in the top-right corner of the header strip, above the main area. The **Logout** button sits next to it.
2. Click **Logout**. Your session ends and the app returns to the **Field Ops Login** panel.

## Tips / common problems
- **Always sign out on a shared or public computer.** The app remembers your sign-in between visits until you log out, so the next person would otherwise land in your account.
- If you're switching users on the same device, click **Logout** first, then sign in with the other account — this makes sure the new person's role and screens load cleanly.
