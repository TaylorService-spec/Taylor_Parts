# View a customer's record

**What this lets you do:** See everything about one customer on a single page — summary, contacts, locations, and service activity.

**Who can do it:** Admin and Dispatcher.

**Before you start:** Open the **Customers** list and click a customer's name.

## What you see

The record is laid out top to bottom in sections:

1. **Account Summary** — the customer's name, status and relationship badges, customer number, billing address, primary contact, and tags. An **Edit** button sits here.
2. **Financial Summary** — currently shows "Sales data source not connected." No dollar figures appear yet (see *Financial summary*).
3. **Contacts** — everyone on file for this customer, with an **+ Add Contact** button.
4. **Locations** — the customer's sites, with an **+ Add Location** button.
5. **Service Activity** — Completed and Open work order counts and a timeline of work orders (see *See a customer's work orders and activity*).
6. **Notes & Identifiers** — a collapsed section; click it to expand notes and any external IDs.

## Steps

1. From **Customers**, click a customer's name to open the record.
2. Scroll to the section you need.
3. Click **&larr; Back to Customers** at the top to return to the list.

## Tips and common problems

- If the page says "Customer not found.", the record may have been removed or the link is stale — use **Back to Customers**.
- If a contact section warns that several contacts are marked primary, open **Edit** on each contact source and resolve it to a single primary (see *Add a contact*).
