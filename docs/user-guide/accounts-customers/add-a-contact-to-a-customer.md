# Add a contact to a customer

**What this lets you do:** Record a person to contact at a customer — name, phone, email, and whether they're the primary contact.

**Who can do it:** Admin and Dispatcher.

**Before you start:** Open the customer's record from the **Customers** list.

## Steps

1. On the customer's record, look at the **Contacts** section at the top of the right-hand column.
2. Click **+ Add contact**. A short form appears.
3. Type the contact's **Name** (required), and optionally **Phone** and **Email**.
4. Tick **Primary Contact** if this is the main person to reach.
5. Click **Add Contact**. The new contact appears in the list; a primary contact is shown with a **Primary** badge.

## Tips and common problems

- The contact **Name** is required — a blank name won't save.
- If the Contacts section later warns that several contacts are marked primary, only one should be. Mark just one contact as primary. Until it is resolved, the phone view offers no **Call** button — the app will not pick one of them for you.
- There is no delete or edit button on a contact in this screen yet — add contacts carefully.
