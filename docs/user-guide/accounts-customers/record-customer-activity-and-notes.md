# Record customer activity and notes

**What this lets you do:** Keep a dated log of your interactions with a customer — calls, meetings, sales notes and relationship check-ins — on the customer's record.

**Who can do it:**
- **Seeing the log:** you need access to read CRM activity *and* access to read the customer record itself.
- **Adding a note:** you need access to record CRM activity *and* access to read the customer record.

Today these are held by administrators, the owner, and anyone given the CRM-activity contributor role.

## See the log

1. Open the customer's record.
2. Find the **Activity & Notes** section. The most recent interaction is at the top, with its type, when it happened, who recorded it, and the note.
3. A note logged after the event shows both times: when it happened and when it was recorded.

## Add a note

1. In **Activity & Notes**, click **+ Add Note**.
2. Choose the **Type**: Sales Note, Call, Meeting, Relationship or General.
3. Write the **Note** (up to 4,000 characters).
4. Click **Save Note**. The note appears at the top of the list.

If saving fails and you click **Save Note** again, the note is still saved only once.

## Tips / common problems

- **"You are not authorized to view CRM activity for this account."** Your access does not include reading CRM activity, or does not include reading this customer. Ask an administrator.
- **"Activity & Notes is currently unavailable. Try again later."** The log could not be loaded, or it has more entries than can be shown at once.
- **"No activity logged yet for this account."** The log loaded and is empty.
- **"Please check the note and try again."** The note is empty or too long, or the time you entered is in the future.
- **"Could not save the note."** The note links a contact, opportunity or sales order that does not belong to this customer.
- **Notes recorded before the move to EOS** may not be visible yet. They are copied over separately, once an administrator approves the copy.
