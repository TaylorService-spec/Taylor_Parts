# Browse and search customers

**What this lets you do:** See your list of customers and jump to any one of them.

**Who can do it:** Admin and Dispatcher. (Technicians do not see the Customers area.)

**Before you start:** Sign in. You'll find **Customers** in the top navigation.

## Steps

1. Click **Customers** in the top navigation. You land on the **Customers** list. Each row shows the customer **Name**, a **Status** badge (for example Prospect or Active), and any **Tags**.
2. To find a specific customer, type in the **Search customers...** box at the top. The list narrows as you type.
3. Click a customer's **name** to open their full record.

## Tips and common problems

- If the list says "No customers yet. Create one above.", none have been added — use **+ New Customer** (see *Add a new customer*).
- The other tabs under Customers — **Contacts**, **Locations**, **Equipment**, **Service History** — are not built yet and show a placeholder. Contacts and locations are managed inside each customer's record instead.
