# Set a Customer's Line of Business (Taylor / Ventana)

You can mark whether a customer is a **Taylor** relationship, a **Ventana**
relationship, or **both**. It's optional, it's just for your own reporting and
sales organization, and it doesn't change anyone's permissions or how any
individual order or invoice is handled.

## Set it when creating or editing a customer

1. Open **Customers** and either **+ New Customer** or open a customer and choose
   **Edit**.
2. In the form, find the **Line of Business** section (just below **Relationship**).
3. Tick **Taylor**, **Ventana**, or **both** — whichever applies. Leave both
   unticked if it doesn't apply yet.
4. **Save**.

## Where it shows up

On the customer's record, the Line of Business appears as **badges** next to the
status and Customer/Vendor badges — e.g. a **Taylor** badge, a **Ventana** badge,
or both. A customer with nothing set shows **no badge** (it never silently assumes
"Taylor").

## Good to know

- **Optional and additive.** Existing customers show nothing until you set it —
  there's no bulk change and nothing to clean up.
- **"Both" is normal.** A customer who buys equipment from Ventana and parts from
  Taylor is genuinely both — tick both.
- **It doesn't decide an order's company.** Line of Business is a customer-level
  relationship for reporting and rep assignment. Which company's books a specific
  sale or invoice lands on is decided per transaction, separately — not by this
  setting.
- **It grants no access.** Like the Customer/Vendor relationship, it's
  informational only and never changes what anyone can see or do.
