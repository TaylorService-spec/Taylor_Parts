# See a customer's work orders and activity

**What this lets you do:** See how many work orders a customer has, and a list of them with their status, schedule and technician.

**Who can do it:** Admin and Dispatcher.

**Before you start:** Open the customer's record from the **Customers** list.

## Steps

1. On the customer's record, go to the **Service activity** section in the main column. (On a phone it's near the bottom of the page, after the primary contact.)
2. Beside the heading are two counts: **Open work orders** and **Completed**. Each is counted independently.
3. Below them, work orders are listed newest first. Each row shows the work order number, its **status in words** (Scheduled, Dispatched, On site, Work in progress…), when it is **scheduled**, and which **technician** it is assigned to.
4. Click a work order number to open that work order.
5. If there are more than fit on screen, click **Load More** to show older ones. When there are none left you'll see "End of activity."

## Tips and common problems

- **"No service activity yet for this customer."** means this customer has no work orders.
- **"Not scheduled"** means the work order genuinely has no scheduled date yet — it isn't a loading problem.
- **"Unassigned"** means no technician is on the job. If a technician *is* assigned but their name can't be looked up, you'll see "Unknown technician" rather than an internal ID.
- If a count says **"couldn't be read"**, or the list says **"Service activity couldn't be read"**, the data didn't load — use **Refresh** at the top of the page. The counts and the list load separately, so one can fail without affecting the others.
- **Work orders past their scheduled window** also appear in the **Attention** box at the top of the record, under **Past Due**.
- **Equipment is not listed on this page.** There's no per-customer equipment list yet, and a partial one would mislead — the section links you to the Equipment workspace instead.
