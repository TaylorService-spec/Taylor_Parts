# Add a new customer

**What this lets you do:** Create a new customer record.

**Who can do it:** Admin and Dispatcher.

**Before you start:** Have the customer's name ready. Everything else is optional and can be added later.

## Steps

1. Go to **Customers** in the top navigation.
2. Click **+ New Customer**. A form opens above the list.
3. Type the **Customer name**. This is the only required field.
4. Pick a **Status** from the dropdown (for example Prospect or Active).
5. Under **Relationship**, tick **Customer**, **Vendor**, or both. This is informational only.
6. Optionally fill in the billing address (street, city, state, zip).
7. Optionally add **Notes** (alarm codes, call-ahead requirements, billing reminders) and **Tags** (comma-separated, for example `Restaurant, VIP`).
8. If you need them, click **Show external IDs (future integrations)** to enter a customer number, ERP ID, accounting ID, or legacy ID. These are stored for future system integrations and are not used elsewhere today.
9. Click **Create Customer**. The form closes and the new customer appears in the list.

## Tips and common problems

- If nothing happens when you click Create, check that the **Customer name** isn't blank — a name is required.
- Click **Cancel** to close the form without saving.
