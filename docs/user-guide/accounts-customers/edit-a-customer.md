# Edit a customer

**What this lets you do:** Change a customer's name, status, address, notes, tags, or external IDs.

**Who can do it:** Admin and Dispatcher.

**Before you start:** Open the customer's record from the **Customers** list.

## Steps

1. On the customer's record, click **Edit customer** (top right, beside the customer name).
2. The record turns into the edit form, pre-filled with the current values.
3. Change any field — name, status, relationship, billing address, notes, or tags.
4. To edit external identifiers, click **Show external IDs (future integrations)**.
5. Click **Save Changes**. The form closes and the record shows your updates.

## Tips and common problems

- The **Customer name** cannot be left blank — clear it and Save Changes won't take effect.
- Click **Cancel** to discard your changes and return to the record unchanged.
