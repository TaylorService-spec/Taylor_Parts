# Update a work order's status

**What this lets you do:** Move one of your work orders through its stages — accept it, mark travel and arrival, start the work, and complete it.

**Who can do it:** Technician, on work orders assigned to you.

## Before you start

- Open the work order from **Dashboard > My Dashboard** by selecting its card. The detail view shows the current stage and the buttons available to you right now.
- You can only act on your own assigned work orders. The buttons that appear depend on the current stage, so you always see just the next valid step.

## Steps

1. Select the work order card to open its detail view. Near the top you'll see a status line describing where it stands (for example "Awaiting your acceptance").
2. Select the action button for the next step. Which button appears depends on the stage:
   - **Accept** — when it was just dispatched to you.
   - **Start Travel** — after you've accepted.
   - **Arrived On Site** — once you're traveling.
   - **Start Work** — when you're on site.
   - **Complete Work Order** — when work is in progress.
3. The button shows "Working..." briefly while it saves, then the status line updates to the new stage. Only one action is available at a time — finish one step to reveal the next.
4. When you complete a work order, it moves out of your active sections and appears under **Completed Today**.

## Tips and common problems

- **Only one button shows.** That's expected — you're always offered just the next valid step, not every stage.
- **No action buttons at all.** The work order is at a stage you don't act on (for example already completed, closed, or not yet dispatched to you).
- **A red error appears after you tap.** The change was rejected (for example the work order moved on in another session). Go **Back to list** and reopen the card to see its current stage, then try again.
- Status changes save to the live system, not just your device.
