# Warehouse work with no signal

## What this lets you do

Keep receiving, putting away, counting and moving stock in the parts of the building where your phone
has no signal — and know exactly what has and hasn't reached the office once you're back in range.

## Who can do it

Anyone using the Warehouse app on a phone. You'll only see the tasks your account is actually staffed
for.

## Before you start

Nothing to switch on. The app holds your work automatically whenever it can't reach the platform.

---

## 1. What "waiting to sync" means here

When you scan something with no signal, the app keeps it **on the phone** and marks it
**Waiting to sync**.

That means one thing: your scan is safe on this device, and **the platform has not seen it yet**.
Until it syncs:

- the stock has **not** been received
- nothing has been **put away**
- no transfer has **moved**
- no count has **changed** anything
- no return has been **restocked**

Those are all things only the platform can say, and it hasn't been told yet.

Sync happens when you get signal back, or when you press **Sync now**.

## 2. What you can do with no signal

All of these can be captured offline:

| Task | Held offline |
|---|---|
| Receiving | Yes |
| Put away | Yes |
| Pick and stage | Yes |
| Transfer — send | Yes |
| Transfer — receive | Yes |
| Truck handoff | Yes |
| Cycle count | Yes |
| Return intake | Yes |
| **Reviewing a count (reconciling)** | **No — see section 6** |

## 3. Where to see what's outstanding

- **A banner at the top of Home** tells you how many items are waiting, or how many need you.
- **More → Sync status** opens the full list, with a count beside it.

Press **Sync now** to try sending straight away. Pressing it yourself also skips any waiting period
the app had set for itself after a failure — if you know you have signal, you don't have to wait.

## 4. Two different "statuses"

On the Sync screen you'll see two status lines on the same card, and they mean different things:

- **Transfer status** (or Count status, etc.) — what the **business** record says. For example
  *Cancelled*.
- **Sync status** — what **your phone** has managed to send. For example *Waiting to sync*.

A transfer can be *Requested* while your dispatch is still *Waiting to sync*. Those are two separate
facts, and the screen keeps them separate on purpose.

## 5. When something needs your attention

Each card shows the details as separate fields — Transfer, Source, Destination, Part, Quantity,
Serial — so you can see **which one changed**. Then:

1. **What happened**, in plain terms.
2. **What's preserved** — always: *"Your work is kept on this phone. Nothing has been lost."*
3. **What to do next.**

Your options are **Try again**, **Discard** (asks you to confirm — it can't be undone), and
**Details** for the technical code if you're calling it in.

### The situations you'll actually hit

- **"That transfer was cancelled while you were offline."** Nothing moved. Check with whoever
  cancelled it before you move any stock.
- **"This transfer has not been sent from the other end yet."** Nothing to do. It'll go through once
  the other end dispatches it.
- **"That bin is no longer in use."** Scan a different bin. **The app will never pick another bin for
  you** — don't put the stock down until it's recorded somewhere real.
- **"More was received than the order still expects."** Somebody else received part of it while you
  were offline. Recount what you physically have, then receive the remainder.
- **"That serial number was already received."** Check the unit in your hands — two units can't share
  a serial.
- **"That count was already reviewed and closed."** Your numbers are kept on the phone but can't be
  added now. Show them to your manager.

## 6. Reviewing a count needs a connection

You can **count** with no signal all day. You cannot **review** a count offline, and that's
deliberate — not a limitation waiting to be fixed.

Reviewing means approving a difference between what was expected and what was counted. That's a
decision about numbers as they stand *right now*, and you can't make it on a phone that hasn't seen
them. The app says **"Reconnect to reconcile this count."**

## 7. Returns do not put stock back

Taking a return in **records that it arrived**. It does not put it back into stock, mark it repaired,
scrapped, or credited — offline or online. Those steps don't exist in the system yet, so nothing on
this screen will claim them.

## 8. Two people, one quantity

If you and somebody else both capture a receipt for the same remaining quantity while offline,
**whoever syncs first gets it**. The second one is told the order doesn't expect that much any more.

That's working correctly. The app never holds a quantity for you just because you scanned it — doing
so would mean promising stock the platform never agreed to.

## 9. "This phone is not saving work offline"

If you see this, your phone can't store anything — usually because its storage is full.

**Stay on the screen and get into signal before you carry on.** Anything you scan may be lost if the
app closes. The app will not tell you work is queued when it can't actually keep it.

## 10. Signing out with work waiting

Your queued work belongs to **you**. If somebody else signs in on the same phone they will see their
own queue, not yours — they can't send yours and can't erase it. Sign back in and it's still there.
