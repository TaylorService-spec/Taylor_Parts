## Canonical-concept rules (anti-duplication)
- Work Orders = canonical records/list; Dispatch Board = scheduling workspace over the same records; board/list are views, never separate concepts.
- One Parts concept: workspace (INV-02) + record (INV-03); demand/purchasing is a record section, not a separate destination.
- Accounts is the only customer concept; "Customers List" merges into CRM-01.
- Technician phone experience (TEC-01) is a composition of Service + Inventory surfaces, not a ninth rail group.

## Navigation semantics
- Major record sections are URL-addressable (back/refresh/share/bookmark safe); tabs are styled navigation, not JS state.
- Stable defaults: a record always opens its Overview; role efficiency via explicit contextual deep links (e.g. My Work → Reorder → Part/Demand & Purchasing), never capability-dependent default switching.
- Cross-object continuity links per brief §13 journeys (inbound→invoice; opportunity→customer history; part→consumption history); a user follows the story without returning to the rail.
