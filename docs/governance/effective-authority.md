## Authorized capacity — what a staffed employee could actually do today

**Nine functional Roles are entirely inert.** Every capability they carry is `active:false`, so
assigning one to an employee changes nothing until a separate per-environment activation:

`inventoryLookupReader` (4), `inventoryTransferOperator` (4), `inventoryCycleCountCounter` (3),
`inventoryPutAwayOperator` (2), `inventoryBinAdministrator` (2), `crmActivityContributor` (2),
`inventoryCycleCountReconciler` (1), `inventoryReturnsIntakeClerk` (1), `workOrderPartsPlanner` (1).

This is **fail-closed by design, not a defect** — the capabilities are built and guarded, and the
activation gate is a protected per-environment action. It is stated because a grant plan that assigns
these Roles and expects behaviour would be wrong about its own effect. **Granting them is safe;
expecting them to do anything is not.**

The practical consequence for the workforce population: a Parts Associate composed with Lookup and
Put-away has **5 operable capabilities of 14 granted**, and neither Lookup nor Put-away is among
them. `generalEmployee` holds nothing at all — deliberately, as the floor.

## Four recommendations

### 1. Reporting — 39 capabilities, nobody holds them (LARGEST OPEN GAP)

Every `report.*` id is owner/admin-only. No business Role has ever been assigned Reporting, so
**Reporting is unreachable for every persona** — the same finding Decision #113 recorded about
`owner` having no live assignment.

*Recommendation:* Reporting is **read of data the Role can already see**, not a new authority — a
Sales Manager reading a sales report learns nothing they could not read record by record. Grant
`report.*` **read/run** ids along existing domain visibility (Sales → sales reports, Warehouse →
inventory reports), and keep the **5 W-SAVE definition-CRUD ids owner-only**, because authoring a
report definition *is* a distinct authority. **Owner decision required — do not infer this.**

### 2. Equipment — 5 capabilities, and they are two different questions

`equipment.*` is owner/admin-only, and the five ids are **not one gap**:

- **Four are Equipment Compatibility** — `compatibility.view` / `.import` / `.verify` / `.correct`.
  These belong to the Equipment Compatibility program (D4), whose implementation is still a draft and
  whose alias identity contract is decided but unbuilt. Assigning them would grant authority over an
  engine that is not finished.
- **One is model-catalog administration** — `equipment.model.manage`. This is the id that produced
  one of the three semantic mapping errors this program caught: the CRUD matrix's "edit equipment"
  cell means the **customer's installed base**, and mapping it here would have handed catalog
  administration to technicians, parts associates and shop staff.

*Recommendation:* **split the decision.** Hold the four compatibility ids until D4 ships — there is
nothing to authorize yet. Decide `equipment.model.manage` on its own; the natural holder is whoever
administers the part/model masters (today `inventoryCatalogAdministrator` holds the analogous
`inventory.catalog.manage`), **not** the Service Manager who merely works on equipment. Spec §26.4
recorded this as a catalog gap for `fieldManager`; the gap is real, but the installed base is what
`fieldManager` needs, and the installed base is Rules-governed rather than capability-governed.
**Owner decision required.**

### 3. Coverage / Territory — 2 capabilities, correctly parked

`coverage.*` is owner/admin-only. Commercial Coverage & Territory Management is a recorded roadmap
requirement whose seams are deliberately preserved and whose build is **not** authorized.

*Recommendation:* **leave unassigned.** Assigning them now would model a design that has not been
decided — territory-independent-of-salesperson, multi-assignment resolution, and configurable
channel are all open. This is the one gap where the right action is none.

### 4. Audit Log — assigned, and worth confirming

`audit.event.read` is **not** owner/admin-only: it is what separates the two sales Roles, and the
canonical matrix grants it to the manager layer.

*Recommendation:* **confirm, do not change.** Audit read is oversight, and oversight that only the
Owner can perform is not oversight. Flagged only because a capability that distinguishes two
otherwise-identical Roles deserves to be an intended distinction rather than an accident.
